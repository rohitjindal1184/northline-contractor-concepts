# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React with Vite.

## Users

- Primary business audience: general contractors who do not yet have a credible website and need to see a concrete example of what one could do for their business.
- Primary site visitors: homeowners evaluating a contractor for renovation, addition, or general construction work.

## Product Purpose

Create a reusable fictional contractor website that can be shown during cold outreach. It should feel complete enough that a contractor can picture their own company, projects, and contact details in the same experience.

## Positioning

The page is a sales demonstration disguised as a credible contractor website: it must communicate trust, workmanship, project fit, and a clear estimate path without relying on claims that belong to a real business.

## Operating Context

The site will commonly be opened from a cold email or shown during direct outreach. Visitors may scan it quickly on a phone. The demo must therefore explain the contractor's services, process, and next step immediately.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Use a reusable fictional contractor brand rather than a real company.
- Primary conversion path: request a free estimate.
- Showcase conversion path: schedule a website consultation through the embedded TidyCal booking page.
- The estimate form is a front-end demonstration and must not imply a live CRM or backend.
- Business-specific details such as service area, license number, phone, proof points, and project photography are replaceable placeholders.
- Assumption for this build: the fictional contractor offers renovations, additions, and whole-home construction.

## Brand Commitments

- Fictional, reusable brand; no real contractor identity.
- Plainspoken, capable, trustworthy voice.

## Evidence on Hand

No real testimonials, completed-project metrics, certifications, license details, customer logos, or owned project photography were supplied. The page must not fabricate them as verified commercial proof.

## Product Principles

- Earn trust through clarity, visible craft, and a transparent process.
- Make the estimate request feel simple and low-pressure.
- Keep every business-specific fact easy to replace for a future prospect.
- Prefer believable specificity over generic contractor marketing language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts for mobile and desktop.
